  • FixedDropdown colors in dark mode, and card shadows.
  • ImprovedType and spacing are now consistent across every screen.

Approval and Management portals

  • AddedDark and light mode on all three portals, and links between them.

Coordinator dashboard rebuild

  • AddedA visual itinerary timeline of stops, transport legs, and stays, with in-place editing.
  • AddedTransport details per leg: operator, booking reference, flight or train number, and departure and arrival points and times.

  • AddedDiscard Trip, to permanently remove a trip created by mistake before it's submitted.

  • ImprovedAdd a route stop at any position, and edit dates or route from the header bar.
  • ImprovedDozens of layout and spacing refinements based on day-to-day use.

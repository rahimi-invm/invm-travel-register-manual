01

Overview

Use the Travel Register to declare and get approval for business travel outside Klang Valley. Sign in with your INVM Google account — there's no separate password. What you see depends on your role.

Every trip follows the same path:

  1. A coordinator (the person organizing the trip) creates it in the Coordinator Portal.
  2. Supporting documents are uploaded to the trip's Drive folder.
  3. The coordinator submits the trip for approval.
  4. The assigned approver approves or rejects it.
  5. The coordinator and Management users are emailed the decision.

02

Coordinator Portal

Create and manage your trips here. The sidebar lists your trips, with search and a status filter.

02.1  Create a trip

  1. Click + Add New Trip.
  2. Enter the Trip Name and the start and end dates.
  3. Choose Domestic or International, then a destination: a region and city, or a country. The starting point is preset.
  4. Pick the Trip Activities that apply (use + Other if none fit). Business Category is filled in for you. A Reference / Subject and notes are optional.
  5. Save. The trip is created as Drafting and opens in the review panel.

Add stops, transport, stays, and travelers afterwards, in the review panel.

02.2  Review and edit a trip

Select a trip in the sidebar. The review panel has four parts:

  • Header bar — click the pencil next to the Trip Name, dates, or route to edit it in place.
  • Trip Summary — your itinerary as a timeline of stops, transport legs, and stays. Click a pencil to edit a leg or stay, including flight numbers, times, and hotel details. Use + Add another stay when you change hotels at one destination.
  • Objective & Context — activities, business category, reference/subject, and notes. These are view-only for now; only the Trip Name can be edited (from the header bar).
  • Travelers & Roles — click Manage Travelers, search and click people to add or remove them, then Save. The role dropdown changes who is labelled Coordinator, not who can edit the trip.

02.3  Supporting documents

Each trip has its own Google Drive folder, linked from the review panel. Upload documents there, such as invitation letters or itineraries. At least one document is required before you can submit.

02.4  Submit for approval

Click Submit for Approval. The assigned approver is emailed, and most editing is locked until they decide or you withdraw. It helps to let your approver know directly.

02.5  Actions and reasons

ActionWhenReason
Edit dates, route, transport, or travelersBefore you submit, and after a withdrawal, rejection, or change requestOptional
Withdraw SubmissionWhile waiting for a decisionRequired
Request ChangeAfter approval. Reopens the trip for edits and needs re-approval.Required

Once a trip is Cancelled, Completed, or Archived, none of these actions are available.
03

Approval Portal

The Approvals link appears in your sidebar only if you're a designated approver, and you only see requests assigned to you.

03.1  Review a request

Select a request in the sidebar to review the trip (read-only). Filter by Pending, Approved, or Rejected, or search by trip name or coordinator.

03.2  Approve or reject

Click Approve or Reject. Remarks are optional when approving; rejecting requires a reason so the coordinator knows what to fix. The coordinator and all Management users are emailed your decision.

03.3  Revision history

If a trip is resubmitted after a rejection or change request, each submission is kept as a numbered revision. Open a request to see the status, dates, and remarks of every revision.

04

Management Portal

A read-only view of all travel for Management users, opened from the Management Dashboard link in your sidebar. It doesn't affect your own trips or approvals.

04.1  Dashboard

Choose a period (Month, Range, or Year) to see:

  • Stat cards — Total Trips, Active Now, Pending Approvals, Avg. Approval Turnaround, and Rejection Rate.
  • Breakdowns by status, approval outcome, department, trip type, and category. Click a slice to see the trips behind it.
  • Trips per Month — a trailing 12-month chart that doesn't change with the period.

04.2  Resource Availability

Open the Resource Availability tab to see who's traveling and when. Choose a date range (and optionally a department). Each row is an employee and each bar is a trip, so overlaps are easy to spot. Click a bar for the trip's details.

05

Status Reference

Every trip has two statuses: a Trip Status (where the trip is in its life) and an Approval Status (where it is in the approval process). They appear as the same chips you see in the portals. For example, Upcoming with Approved means the trip is ready to go; Drafting with Rejected means it needs changes.

05.1  Trip Status

DraftingBeing prepared or edited: not submitted yet, or sent back after a rejection, withdrawal, or change request.
UpcomingSubmitted or approved, and not started yet.
OngoingTrip in progress.
CompletedTrip finished. If a traveller returns later than the rest, it stays Ongoing until they're back.
CancelledTrip cancelled. The record is kept.
ArchivedA Completed trip, moved out of the default list 30 days later. Nothing is deleted.

05.2  Approval Status

UnsubmittedNot submitted yet.
ReviewingWaiting for the approver's decision (first submission or resubmission).
ApprovedApproved.
RejectedRejected. Revise the trip and resubmit.
WithdrawnYou withdrew the submission. Edit and resubmit when ready.
ReopenedYou requested a change to an approved trip. Edit it and resubmit for approval.
06

FAQ

Why don't I see the Approvals link?

It appears only for designated approvers. If you should be one, ask whoever maintains the employee list.

What's the difference between Discard and Cancel?

Discard permanently deletes a trip that was never submitted. Cancel keeps the record and needs a reason; use it once a trip has been submitted.

Why can't I edit a field?

Most fields lock while a trip is waiting for approval or approved. Use Withdraw Submission or Request Change to unlock them. Also, only the Trip Name is editable in Objective & Context for now; activities, category, reference/subject, and notes are view-only.

Why did an old trip disappear from my list?

Completed trips are Archived automatically after 30 days and leave the default list. Nothing is deleted.

I'm a Management user and a coordinator. Do I lose my trips?

No. Being a Management user only adds the Management Dashboard link. Your own trips are unaffected.

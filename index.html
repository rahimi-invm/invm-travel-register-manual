01

Overview

A trip starts either as a Google Form submission or a New Trip in the Coordinator Portal — both produce an identical record: a unique Travel ID, a participant list, a route, and a Google Drive folder for supporting documents. From there:

  1. The coordinator reviews and refines the trip's details.
  2. Supporting documents are uploaded to the trip's Drive folder.
  3. The coordinator submits the trip for approval.
  4. The assigned approver reviews it and approves or rejects.
  5. The coordinator — and anyone management-flagged — is emailed the outcome automatically.

02

Signing In

All three portals use your existing INVM Google account — there's no separate password to remember. Whichever portal link you open, what you see is scoped to your signed-in email: coordinators see their own trips, approvers see only requests assigned to them, and management-flagged users additionally get a Management Dashboard link.

03

Coordinator Portal

Your home base for creating and managing trips. The sidebar lists every trip you've submitted or created, with search and a status filter.

03.1  Creating a New Trip

  1. Click + Add New Trip.
  2. Fill in Trip Name and dates. Origin is fixed to your home base.
  3. Pick Trip Activities from the chip picker — Business Category is derived automatically from what you select (use + Other if nothing fits).
  4. Choose your destination — a curated region → city dropdown for domestic trips, or a country dropdown for international ones.
  5. Save. The trip lands in Drafting, and the review panel opens automatically.

Route detail (stops, transport) and traveler assignment happen after creation, in the review panel below — not in the creation form itself.

03.2  Reviewing & Editing a Trip

  • Header bar — Trip Name, dates, and route each have a pencil icon for a quick inline edit, without leaving the page.
  • Trip Summary — the itinerary as a vertical timeline: each stop, transport leg, and stay in order, connected by a line. Click a pencil to edit a leg or stay in place; use + Add another stay for a split-hotel destination.
  • Objective & Context — Trip Activities, Business Category, Reference/Subject, and Remarks, shown as reference. Only Trip Name is editable today (via the header-bar pencil); the rest is display-only for now.
  • Travelers & Roles — click Manage Travelers, then click a person's chip to add or remove them. Each traveler's role dropdown can reassign who's shown as Coordinator — this only changes the display label, not who has edit access to the trip.

03.3  Supporting Documents

Every trip gets its own Drive folder, linked from the review panel. Upload invitation letters, itineraries, or approvals there directly in Drive — there's no separate uploader inside the portal. At least one document is required before you can submit for approval.

03.4  Submitting for Approval

Once the trip is ready and has at least one document, click Submit for Approval. This sends the request to the assigned approver, emails them, and locks the fields listed below until the approver decides (or you withdraw).

03.5  Trip Actions & Reasons

04

Approval Portal

You'll only see an Approvals link in your own Coordinator sidebar if you're listed as someone's designated approver — the portal itself only ever shows requests assigned to your email.

04.1  Reviewing a Request

Open a request from the sidebar list to see a read-only view of the trip — the same layout coordinators see, minus any edit controls. Filter the list by Pending, Approved, or Rejected, or search by trip name or coordinator.

04.2  Approve or Reject

Use the Approve or Reject button on the request. Rejecting requires a short reason so the coordinator knows what to fix; approving doesn't. Either way, the coordinator and every management-flagged user get an automatic email with the decision, your remarks, and next steps.

04.3  Revision History

If a trip is resubmitted after a rejection or a requested change, each version is tracked as its own revision. Open the request to see the full history — every past submission's status, dates, and remarks — not just the current one.

05

Management Portal

A read-only oversight view, visible only to management-flagged users via the Management Dashboard link. It's independent of whether you're also a coordinator or approver — nothing about your own trips changes.

05.1  Dashboard

Pick a period (month, custom range, or year) to see stat cards — total trips, active now, pending approvals, average approval turnaround — plus breakdowns by status, approval outcome, department, trip type, and category. Click any breakdown slice to drill into the underlying trip list.

05.2  Resource Availability

Switch to the Resource Availability tab for a forward-looking calendar of who's traveling and when — one row per employee, one bar per trip, so overlapping travel is visible at a glance. Click a bar to drill into that specific trip.

06

Status Reference

Trip Status and Approval Status are two separate fields that change independently — a trip can be Upcoming and Rejected at the same time (submitted, then rejected, not yet re-edited).

06.1  Trip Status

DraftingEditable, not yet submitted for approval.
UpcomingSubmitted and not yet started, regardless of decision.
OngoingTrip currently in progress.
CompletedFinished — stays open until the last traveller's return date, including any override.
CancelledTrip cancelled.
ArchivedAged out of the active list, automatically, 30 days after Completed. The record itself is unchanged — it just drops off the default view.

06.2  Approval Status

UnsubmittedNot yet submitted for approval.
ReviewingAwaiting a decision — initial submission or a resubmission.
ApprovedApproved.
RejectedRejected — coordinator can revise and resubmit.
WithdrawnSubmission withdrawn by the coordinator.
ReopenedAn approved trip needs changes before it can proceed.
07

FAQ

Why don't I see the Approvals link?

It only appears if you're listed as someone's designated approver. If you believe you should be one, check with whoever maintains the employee list.

What's the difference between Discard and Cancel?

Discard permanently deletes the trip and its records — only available before it's ever been submitted, no reason needed beyond a confirmation. Cancel keeps the full record for audit and requires a reason — it's available any time before the trip reaches a terminal status.

Why can't I edit some fields on my trip?

Trip Name can be edited from the header bar. The rest of Objective & Context (Business Category, Reference/Subject, Additional Notes) doesn't have an edit control in the portal yet — if you need one of those changed, flag it directly for now.

Why did my old trip disappear from the list?

Completed trips move to Archived automatically 30 days after completion and drop off the default view. Nothing is deleted — the record is unchanged.

How do I upload a document?

Open the trip's Drive folder link from the review panel and upload it there directly — the portal doesn't have its own uploader. At least one document is required before you can submit for approval.

I'm a management-flagged coordinator — do I lose my own trip list?

No. Being management-flagged only adds the Management Dashboard link; your own "My Trips" view is completely unaffected.
